## TriviaQuiz

TriviaQuiz is a modern, offline-first Android trivia game designed with engaging mechanics and a comprehensive question bank across 13 diverse categories.

### Key Features
- **13 Diverse Categories**: Science, history, sports, technology, geography, and general knowledge.
- **Customizable Game Modes**: Adjustable timer, question count, and difficulty tiers.
- **Google Play Games Integration**: XP progression, level milestones, and competitive leaderboards.
