## Project Overview

**AstroYorum** blends astronomical calculations with modern artificial intelligence to deliver an immersive astrology and divination experience. From complex natal birth chart calculations to daily horoscopes and interactive tarot spreads, it provides rich, personalized cosmic guidance.

### Architecture & Technical Design

- **UI & Animations:** Pure **Jetpack Compose** interface styled with cosmic visual themes and responsive dark mode palettes.
- **Natal Chart Computation:** Deterministic planetary calculations mapping exact celestial coordinates based on birth time and location.
- **Cloud Infrastructure:** Integrated with Firebase Firestore and Authentication for secure cross-device profile synchronization.
- **Monetization Architecture:** Non-intrusive AdMob banner and rewarded video ad integration conforming to Google Play policies.

### Key Capabilities

1. **Detailed Birth Charts:** Comprehensive houses, rising signs, Sun, and Moon placements with astrological breakdowns.
2. **AI-Assisted Tarot Spreads:** Classical Rider-Waite symbolism translated into insightful AI interpretations.
3. **Daily & Weekly Transits:** Real-time updates corresponding to actual celestial alignments.
