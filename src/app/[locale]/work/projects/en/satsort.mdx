## SatSort

SatSort is a visual channel list organizer for Linux designed to edit, sort, and clean SatcoDX (`.sdx`) files for smart TVs and satellite receivers.

### Key Features
- **Drag-and-Drop Reordering**: Move and shift channel positions seamlessly with zero sequence drift.
- **Batch Cleanup Tools**: Remove encrypted channels, sort radios to the bottom, and eliminate duplicates.
- **Multi-Format Export**: Export cleaned lists to CSV, TXT, and M3U playlists for VLC / TVheadend.
