## NotesAssistant

NotesAssistant is an AI-assisted note-taking app designed to streamline personal organization and thought management.

### The Approach

- By leveraging AI prompting, I established a Kotlin-based Android application.
- The product focuses on an intuitive UI that was created based on my design specifications.
- This project highlights the process of taking a utility concept and managing its development by orchestrating artificial intelligence.
