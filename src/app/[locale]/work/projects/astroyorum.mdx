## AstroYorum

AstroYorum is an Astrology and Tarot application conceptualized and developed through AI assistance. 

### Development Process
As an AI-Assisted Developer, I managed the development of this app:
- **Architecture**: Directed AI tools to build a Kotlin and Jetpack Compose structure.
- **Backend & Monetization**: Integrated Firebase for backend services and AdMob for monetization.
- **The Vision**: The logic, user flow, and product vision were designed by me, while the code generation was facilitated by AI models.
