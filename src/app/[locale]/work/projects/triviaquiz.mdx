## Project Overview

**TriviaQuiz** is an expansive, offline-first trivia game delivering an entertaining knowledge experience with over 26,000 verified questions in Turkish. Designed with Google Play Games Services integration, it allows players to unlock achievements and compete on global leaderboards without requiring an active internet connection.

### Architecture & Technical Design

- **Architecture:** Clean Architecture, Single Activity design, and predictable MVI (Model-View-Intent) state management.
- **Modern UI:** Built entirely in **Jetpack Compose** with fluid question transitions, reactive timers, and dynamic color palettes.
- **Optimized Question Engine:** High-speed SQLite queries capable of retrieving randomized questions in sub-milliseconds.
- **Play Games Integration:** Global leaderboards and category-specific milestone achievements.

### Key Capabilities

1. **13 Broad Categories:** History, Geography, Cinema, Science, Literature, Sports, and more.
2. **True Offline Play:** Seamless experience on subways, flights, or anywhere without cell reception.
3. **Lifeline System:** 50/50, question pass, and time extensions for engaging gameplay.
