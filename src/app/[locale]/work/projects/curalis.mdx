## Project Overview

**Curalis** is a modern Android application engineered to help individuals manage their medications, dosage schedules, and health metrics with uncompromising privacy. Built entirely on an offline-first foundation, it keeps all sensitive health records encrypted locally on the device without ever transmitting data to third-party cloud servers.

### Architecture & Technical Design

- **Modern Android Architecture:** Rooted in Clean Architecture and MVVM design patterns for testability and maintainability.
- **User Experience:** Fluid animations, dynamic theming, and Material 3 design built with pure **Jetpack Compose**.
- **Local Data Storage:** Encrypted relational storage using **Room DB** (SQLite) directly on the client device.
- **Background Scheduling:** Resilient, battery-friendly reminders powered by **WorkManager** and exact AlarmManager APIs.

### Key Capabilities

1. **Intelligent Medication Schedule:** Complex dosage intervals, custom cycle recurring hours, and flexible intake routines.
2. **Personal Health Journal:** Track blood pressure, glucose levels, heart rate, and symptoms with visual trend analytics.
3. **Refill & Inventory Warnings:** Early alerts for critical supply depletion.
4. **Absolute Zero Data Collection:** No analytics, telemetries, or trackers; your medical records remain strictly private.
